PVE+NPC Cluster

A PVE cluster with the addition of NPC's. Using a mod called "Human NPCs ASA", created by ZoneKiller, you can encounter other human tribes, which you can raid for loot. You can also tame NPC humans to have them help out with various tasks around your base. Not all of the humans are friendly, though, and may try and attack your base. That means you'll need to be more strategic with how you build and utilize more of the game components previously only useful in PvP modes. Human NPC's will not foundation wipe or steal any items on raids and will not attack any passive tames. They will, however, attack anything that attacks them.


Hybrid PVP Cluster

A hybrid, fast-paced PvP experience with 90-day wipe cycles. Build your base in peace for 12 days before PvP combat is enabled. For an entire weekend, tribes are free to raid others, or stay back and defend their own bases. This hybrid approach combines the thrill of PvP with the strategic depth of base building, ensuring that both offensive and defensive skills are constantly tested. The wipe cycle ensures that no one tribe can ever dominate the map and all players, new or returning, will have a fair chance to compete. This would also lead to more dynamic and engaging gameplay experiences for all participants. Allowing players to equally build up before combat provides an opportunity for more strategic and dynamic battles between players on a more level playing field.
